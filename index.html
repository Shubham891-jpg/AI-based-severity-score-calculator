Calculation Mechanics

IT ticket descriptions undergo text pre-processing (token cleaning, punctuation removal, case normalization) and language detection prior to modeling.

Rating Thresholds:
- High (90.0 - 100.0): Complete outages or critical functional blockers.
- Medium (80.0 - 89.9): Severe degradation affecting workflows.
- Low (10.0 - 79.9): Minor incidents, local hardware, or requests.

Numerical ratings are calculated using random forest regressors mapped to 384-dimensional sentence vectors. Confidence ratings capture tree prediction variance.
